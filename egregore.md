# Egregore

## Identity

Egregore's own community — the space where builders, early users, and curious
explorers shape the product together. Part lab, part village, part feedback loop.

We're building a shared intelligence layer for organizations using Claude Code.
This egregore is where the tool gets built, tested, and refined by the people
who use it.

## Culture

Async-first, session-based, radical transparency. Work is captured in artifacts
and handed off between sessions. Decisions are logged. Questions are asked in
the open. Nothing is precious — everything evolves.

Ship early, learn fast. Every session should leave the system a little smarter
than it was before.
